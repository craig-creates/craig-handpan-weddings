# Soulful Wedding Sounds

Build a professional, elegant, conversion-focused website for a UK-based handpan musician offering live music for weddings.

The website should feel calm, premium, soulful, intimate, and trustworthy. It should appeal to couples planning weddings who want something beautiful, unusual, emotional, and atmospheric, but who still need to feel confident that the musician is reliable, polished, punctual, and easy to book.

Avoid making the site feel too hippy, mystical, psychedelic, festival-like, or overly spiritual. The tone should be warm, grounded, refined, natural, and emotionally intelligent.

Brand positioning:

Live handpan music for soulful weddings, ceremonies, drinks receptions, outdoor weddings, elopements, and intimate celebrations.

Main target areas:

Kent, Canterbury, London, Sussex, Surrey, and surrounding areas.

Primary website goal:

Get couples to enquire about availability for their wedding date.

Secondary goals:

- Let visitors hear and understand the sound of the handpan quickly.

- Explain where handpan music fits into a wedding day.

- Present clear wedding packages.

- Build trust and professionalism.

- Make the contact/enquiry process simple.

Suggested site structure:

1. Home

2. Weddings

3. Packages

4. Listen / Watch

5. About

6. FAQ

7. Contact / Check Availability

Design style:

Use warm neutral colours such as cream, stone, sage, soft gold, and earthy brown. Use elegant typography, lots of white space, large atmospheric images, rounded buttons, and a refined countryside wedding aesthetic. The site should feel like: countryside wedding, intimate ceremony, refined bohemian, grounded musician. It should not feel like a backpacker jam circle or psychedelic retreat page.

About page:

Create a concise, wedding-relevant About page. Do not make it too long or overly personal.

Suggested copy:

I’m Craig, a handpan musician creating calm, atmospheric live music for weddings and intimate events. My background spans music, teaching, retreats, and live performance, which has shaped the way I approach wedding music: emotionally present, unobtrusive, and carefully held.

The handpan works beautifully when a couple wants something more personal than a playlist, softer than a band, and more unusual than traditional ceremony music.

FAQ page:

Include these questions and answers:

1. What parts of the wedding can you play for?

Ceremony, guest arrival, aisle walk, signing, exit, drinks reception, wedding breakfast, elopements, and outdoor celebrations.

2. Can you play specific songs?

Some melodies can be adapted, but the handpan works best as atmospheric, melodic live music rather than exact covers. If you have a specific song in mind, I can advise whether it will translate well.

3. Do you need power?

Usually no, unless amplification is required for a larger space.

4. Can you play outdoors?

Yes, weather permitting. A covered or shaded area is needed in rain, strong sun, or difficult weather.

5. How much space do you need?

Very little. A chair or stool and a small performance area are usually enough.

6. Are you insured?

Add placeholder copy for public liability insurance.

7. How far do you travel?

Kent, London, Sussex, Surrey, and surrounding areas. Further travel may be available by arrangement.

8. How do bookings work?

Enquiry, availability check, short call, deposit, confirmation, final details before the wedding.
